FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What Would You Like to Know?

What types of manpower can we request?

Depending on the agency’s confirmed service scope, requests may include technical professionals, skilled trades, operators, administrative staff and other workforce categories. Share your requirements so the team can review them.

Can you support bulk recruitment?

Bulk recruitment requests can be reviewed based on job categories, quantities, candidate availability and required timelines.

How do we request a quotation?

Use the Request Manpower form and provide your company details, roles, workforce quantity, location and expected start date.

How long does recruitment take?

Timing depends on the position, candidate availability, screening, documentation and any required approvals. A realistic timeline can be discussed after the requirement is reviewed.

Do you guarantee selection or deployment?

No outcome should be considered guaranteed. Recruitment and deployment depend on candidate suitability, employer decisions, contractual arrangements and applicable approvals.
